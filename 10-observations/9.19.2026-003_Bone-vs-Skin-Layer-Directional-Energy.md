Observation 4
Recurring pattern (previously noted in other entries): when grabbing an object or moving a limb, the responsive sensation still feels like it originates at the outer skin layer, rather than from a fully prepared bone/limb structure initiating the movement from within.

Possible explanations considered:
- Movement/attention may be indirectly guided by the eyes or other body regions.
- Habitual, unconscious concentration and positive pressure may default to certain (likely superficial) areas during daily activity, sitting, or reflexive/quick movements.
- The limb or region may not be actively "prepared" internally before movement is initiated, so the skin layer becomes the apparent point of activation by default.
