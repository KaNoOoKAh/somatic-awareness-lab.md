### 2. Micro-Localized Gas Expansion & Volumetric Absorption
- **Slow/Shallow Pharyngeal Intake:** 
  - Allows for precise, localized tracking of the air column along the immediate upper airway pathways.
  - Enables micro-expansion and contraction of the localized tissue surrounding the air column.
  - Correlates with a perceived increase in localized, high-efficiency oxygen absorption.
- **Rapid Deep Inhalation (Pulmonary/Abdominal Target):**
  - Air rapidly bypasses the upper airway and fills the lower lungs and abdomen.
  - Requires a conscious, bottom-up mechanical compression (starting from the stomach during exhalation) to process and absorb the gas contents ($O_2$ / $CO_2$).
  - Results in a perception of lower overall volumetric efficiency per breath, requiring heavy concentration to optimize content absorption.
