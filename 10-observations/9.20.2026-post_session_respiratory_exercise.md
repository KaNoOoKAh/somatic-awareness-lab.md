## Observation 16: Morning Deep Nasal Obstruction Following Respiratory Mapping Session

Time: Upon Waking (Exact Time Not Recorded)
Date: 2026-09-20
Location: Honolulu, Oahu, Hawaii

### Context

This observation occurred following the previous evening's breathing, airway-awareness, expansion, contraction, and body-mapping exercises.

The previous session included:

- Throat-focused inhalation through the nose.
- Expansion and contraction of regions below the throat.
- Increased awareness of deep nasal passages.
- Increased awareness of throat and chest regions.
- Development of a more detailed internal map of the respiratory pathway.

### Observation

Upon waking, a significant obstruction was noticed within the nasal passage.

### Description

- The nose felt completely blocked.
- The perceived location of the obstruction was deeper within the nasal cavity rather than at the nostril opening.
- The area appeared to be near the region where airflow is perceived to travel between the nasal passages and the roof of the mouth.
- The blockage was immediately noticeable upon waking.

### Findings

- The sensation was localized deeper within the nasal passage.
- The obstruction felt different from awareness previously experienced near the nostril openings.
- The location appeared to correspond with regions that received significant attention during the previous session.

### Learning

The observation suggests that changes in nasal sensation can occur following intensive respiratory-awareness exercises.

At this stage, the observation documents only the timing and location of the sensation.

### Interpretation

The relationship between the previous session and the morning obstruction is currently unknown.

This observation establishes:

- The breathing and awareness exercises occurred.
- A deep nasal obstruction was present upon waking.

This observation does not establish:

- That the exercises caused the obstruction.
- That airway structure changed.
- That airflow pathways were modified.

Further observations are required to determine whether this pattern repeats across future sessions.

### Importance

This observation is considered significant because:

- The perceived obstruction occurred in a region that received substantial attention during the previous session.
- The sensation was noticed immediately upon waking.
- The location appears deeper than previously documented nasal sensations.

### Confidence

Medium

### Verification Required

Yes

### Follow-Up Questions

- Was the obstruction present on the left side, right side, or both?
- Did it resolve on its own?
- Did changing body position affect it?
- Did the obstruction change after standing, walking, eating, or drinking?
- How long did it last?
- Did normal breathing through the nose return?

## Observation 17: Restoration of Nasal Patency During Exclusive Mouth Breathing

Time: Approximately 00:24 HST
Date: 2026-09-20
Location: Honolulu, Oahu, Hawaii

### Context

This observation occurred after waking and after documenting a deep nasal obstruction within the nasal cavity.

For approximately the previous 30 minutes:

- Breathing was performed exclusively through the mouth.
- No intentional inhalation through the nose was performed.
- No intentional exhalation through the nose was performed.
- Attention remained focused on bodily sensations and respiratory awareness.

### Observation

During the period of exclusive mouth breathing, gradual changes in sensation were noticed across the lower face.

### Description

- Sensation and awareness appeared to return progressively from the lower portions of the face upward.
- The change appeared to occur gradually rather than suddenly.
- The perceived deep nasal obstruction became less noticeable over time.
- By the end of the observation period, the previously obstructed nasal passage no longer felt blocked.
- This change occurred despite the nose not being actively used for breathing during the observation period.

### Findings

- Mouth breathing was maintained for approximately 30 minutes.
- Awareness changes appeared to progress from lower facial regions toward upper facial regions.
- The previously perceived deep obstruction resolved during the observation period.
- Normal perception of the nasal passage returned without intentional nasal breathing.

### Learning

Changes in perceived nasal openness may occur independently of active nasal breathing.

At present, the observation only establishes the sequence of events:

1. Deep nasal obstruction was noticed upon waking.
2. Mouth breathing was maintained for approximately 30 minutes.
3. Facial sensation appeared to change over time.
4. The perceived obstruction resolved.

### Interpretation

This observation does not establish that mouth breathing caused the obstruction to resolve.

However, it documents a temporal relationship between:

- A period of exclusive mouth breathing.
- Progressive changes in facial sensation.
- Restoration of subjective nasal patency.

Additional observations are required to determine whether this pattern repeats in future sessions.

### Importance

This observation is considered significant because it occurred immediately following:

- Observation 16 (deep nasal obstruction upon waking).
- The previous session's respiratory awareness and airway mapping exercises.

The observation may provide useful comparison data for future morning-after sessions.

### Confidence

Medium

### Verification Required

Yes

### Follow-Up Questions

- Was the restored nasal passage equally open on both sides?
- Did nasal breathing feel different after the obstruction resolved?
- Did the sensation return later in the day?
- Did body position influence the change?
- Does the same pattern occur after future respiratory-awareness sessions?

### Session Note

This observation documents a transition from perceived deep nasal obstruction to perceived nasal openness without active nasal respiration during the intervening period.
