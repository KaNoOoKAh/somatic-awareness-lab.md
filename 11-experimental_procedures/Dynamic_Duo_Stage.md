Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Dual-Vector Environmental Optimization
Document ID: EXP-BIO-AERO-001
Version: 1.0

Objective: To systematically evaluate the impact of inverted dynamic lighting profiles and reversed aerodynamic thermal stratification on human circadian biology and indoor climate efficiency.

1. Experimental Overview & Hypotheses

Hypothesis A (Chronobiological)
Exposing the human optic nerve to a high-intensity, short-wavelength blue light spectrum (6500K) during daytime hours will maximize melatonin suppression, accelerating morning alertness. Conversely, transitioning to a low-intensity, long-wavelength amber spectrum (2500K) at night will remove blue-light interference, reducing sleep latency by stimulating natural melatonin production.

Hypothesis B (Therodynamic / Aerodynamic)

Utilizing a counterclockwise (downdraft) blade pitch during cooling periods leverages the wind-chill effect to accelerate skin evaporative cooling. Utilizing a low-speed, clockwise (updraft) blade pitch during heating periods leverages displacement mechanics to destroy thermal stratification, forcing trapped ceiling heat down into the living zone without creating a draft.

2. Equipment & Environmental Controls
• Variable Spectrum Lighting System: Tunable smart LED bulbs or fixtures capable of isolated output at exactly 2500 Kelvin and 6500 Kelvin.
• Reversible Multi-Speed Ceiling Fan: An overhead fan assembly featuring a manual or digital mechanical polarity switch to invert blade rotational direction.
• Environmental Logging Core: The centralized data matrices included in the research appendix to capture daily biological and physical metrics.

3. Standard Step-by-Step Procedure

Phase I: The Daytime Configuration (Active Mode)
This configuration must be deployed within 30 minutes of waking and maintained until 18:00 (6:00 PM).

1. Lighting Alignment: Access your lighting controller and adjust all primary ambient bulbs to 6500 Kelvin (Daylight). Ensure brightness is set to maximum comfortable operating capacity (80% - 100%).
2. Aerodynamic Alignment (Cooling/Summer): Locate the fan polarity switch. Ensure the fan is set to rotate counterclockwise.
3. Speed Regulation: Set the fan velocity to Medium or High. Verify that a continuous, direct downward air column is felt directly beneath the blades.
4. Data Logging: Document morning fatigue levels and initial thermal comfort on your track sheet.

#### Phase II: The Nighttime Configuration (Restoration Mode)


This configuration must be deployed at exactly 18:00 (6:00 PM) and maintained until sleep initialization.

Phase II: 

The Nighttime Configuration (Restoration Mode)
This configuration must be deployed at exactly 18:00 (6:00 PM) and maintained until sleep initialization.

1. Lighting Alignment: Shift all primary ambient bulbs to 2500 Kelvin (Warm Amber). Drop total brightness down to a dimmed state (10% - 30%) to mimic natural twilight.
2. Aerodynamic Alignment (Heating/Winter): Invert the fan polarity switch to rotate clockwise.
3. Speed Regulation: Set the fan velocity strictly to Low. Verify that cool air is being drawn upward toward the ceiling and that no direct downward breeze is felt in the seating area.
4. Data Logging: Complete evening logging parameters, noting sleep latency (time it takes to fall asleep) and overall perceived room warmth before sleeping.
5. Safety & Verification Rules
• Zero Blue-Light Threshold: No raw 6500K light sources may be activated during the nighttime restoration phase. This includes unshielded smartphone or television screens.
• Velocity Restriction: Clockwise fan operation must never be run on high speed during experimental trials, as high-velocity updrafts will overflow the ceiling boundary and create a cooling draft, falsifying thermal results.
