# Spinal Awareness Alignment Protocol

Date:2026-9-20

## Purpose

To increase awareness of spinal positioning and whole-body alignment.

## Procedure

1. Stand on a stable surface.
2. Allow arms to hang naturally.
3. Allow neck to settle naturally.
4. Inhale through mouth.
5. Exhale through mouth.
6. Alternate contraction and relaxation.
7. Physically sending energy/signals through the body and up to the brain.
8. Direct attention through the spine.
9. Maintain awareness while adjusting posture.
10. Record observations.
