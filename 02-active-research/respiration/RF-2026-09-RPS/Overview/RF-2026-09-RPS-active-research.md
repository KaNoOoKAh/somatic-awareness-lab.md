# Respiratory Phase Separation

## Objective

To investigate whether inhalation, relaxation, contraction, and exhalation can be consciously experienced and practiced as separate processes.

## Research Question

Can repeated practice improve the ability to differentiate and control individual phases of breathing?

## Procedure

1. Inhale
2. Relax
3. Apply inward-directed contraction
4. Relax
5. Exhale
6. Apply inward-directed contraction
7. Relax
8. Repeat

## Observation Targets

- Breathing awareness
- Phase separation
- Throat awareness
- Facial awareness
- Chest awareness
- Ease of control
- Ease of relaxation
