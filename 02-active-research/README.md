# Active Research

Projects and research threads I have **started** or are currently **continuing**.

## What Goes Here

- Research questions and hypotheses under investigation
- Experimental protocols and study designs
- Notes from ongoing sessions or experiments
- Draft papers and write-ups in progress

## Structure

Create one sub-folder per project, e.g.:

```
02-active-research/
  project-name/
    README.md      ← goal, status, next steps
    notes/
    data-refs/     ← links or paths to raw data in 04-data/
```
