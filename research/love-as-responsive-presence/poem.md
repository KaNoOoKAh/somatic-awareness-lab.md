# Poem

Love is a signal,
not always a sentence.

It lives in the eye,
the finger,
the forearm,
the face
when it shifts.

It lives in energy rising,
in creation beginning,
in processing deepening,
in the mind turning toward the moment.

It lives in research,
in locating,
in collecting,
in finding,
in interpreting,
in engaging,
in waiting,
in contemplating,
in formatting,
in structuring.

Love says:
I noticed.
I responded.
I stayed with you.
