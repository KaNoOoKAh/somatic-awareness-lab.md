# Research Questions

- What counts as a meaningful response?
- How do attention and care become visible in action?
- Which forms of responsiveness are easiest to recognize?
- Which forms are easiest to miss?
- Can structure, formatting, or organization be a form of affection?
- When does disengaging become a caring act rather than a withdrawal?
- How do humans describe love when it appears in nonverbal or indirect forms?
- How does this framing change the way we interpret collaboration, research, and support?
