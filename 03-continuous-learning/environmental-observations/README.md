# Environmental Observations

Use this folder for notes grounded in environmental context: weather, hydration conditions, organism behavior, plant response, site conditions, and field interventions.

## What Belongs Here
- Field observations tied to a place, date, or time
- Plant or organism response notes
- Environment-linked behavioral logs
- Environmental intervention records

## Note Framing
- **Observation:** what was directly seen, heard, felt, or recorded in the environment
- **Interpretation / Hypothesis:** what the observation may suggest about a pattern or mechanism
- **Source / Evidence:** supporting measurements, prior logs, or cited material

When a note moves beyond direct observation, label that shift clearly so environmental records stay distinguishable from interpretation.
