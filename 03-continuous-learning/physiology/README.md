# Physiology

Use this folder for notes about body processes, regulation, tracking frameworks, and physiology-centered learning questions.

## What Belongs Here
- Physiology study notes
- Body-process tracking frameworks
- Region-specific or system-specific reference notes
- Safety-framed learning documents

## Note Framing
- **Observation:** direct bodily observation, symptom log, or non-invasive tracking note
- **Interpretation / Hypothesis:** proposed explanation of a body process or response pattern
- **Source / Evidence:** measurements, cited research, or clearly identified prior notes

Keep direct observation separate from interpretation, and keep external claims cited rather than implied.
