# Somatic Reflections

Use this folder for reflective notes about writing, behavior, attention, embodied process, and exploratory sense-making.

## What Belongs Here
- Writing and expression reflections
- Behavior-pattern notes
- Embodied process reflections
- Exploratory theory notes that are not yet structured as field logs or physiology references

## Note Framing
- **Observation:** direct personal experience or specific event record
- **Interpretation / Hypothesis:** proposed meaning, pattern, or mechanism
- **Source / Evidence:** prior notes, examples, measurements, or citations when outside research is referenced

Personal reflections can remain open-ended, but observations should stay identifiable and research claims should be cited when used.
