📝 The Official Trial Protocol: Dual-Zone Feedback Brushing

Objective: To master a personalized brushing technique by using the lower jaw's natural mobility, intentional head positioning, and focused biofeedback to achieve a perfectly clean mouth without causing tissue strain.

🔬 What Exactly Are We Doing?

Instead of relying on rapid hand and wrist movements to scrub your teeth, this method splits your mouth into two distinct sensory zones. It uses your body's internal awareness to guide both direction and pressure.

🦷 Zone 1: The Lower Jaw (Active Mobility & Direct Pressure)

• The Action: The hand holds the toothbrush perfectly still in one designated spot.

• The Mechanism: The lower jaw moves independently on its own tracks—sliding forward, backward, or side-to-side against the stationary bristles.

• The Feedback: Because the lower jaw is actively moving, it directly receives, measures, and feels the counter-pressure of the bristles, letting you know exactly how hard you are pressing.

🪐 Zone 2: The Upper Jaw (Spatial Positioning & Conscious Head Tilt)

• The Action: Because the upper jaw is fixed to the skull, it cannot move on its own. Instead, you must visually look at the toothbrush, mentally plan its position, and explicitly tell your body to tilt or turn your head.

• The Mechanism: You manipulate the angle of the brush by changing the orientation of your entire head, matching the stationary brush with deliberate, conscious movements.

• The Feedback: You rely on external visual cues (like a mirror) and internal neck positioning to carefully gauge and vary the different forms of pressure across your top teeth.

🎛️ The Variables We Are Testing

1. Directional Control: Finding the exact angles needed to clear the gum lines and deep grooves by moving the jaw or tilting the head.

2. Pressure Modulation: Learning to read the tactile "feedback" from your teeth and gums so you can instantly soften or firm up the pressure as needed.
