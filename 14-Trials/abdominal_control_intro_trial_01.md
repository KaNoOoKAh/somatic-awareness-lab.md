# Abdominal Control Introductory Trial  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Type:** Somatic Experimental Trial  
**Media:** https://youtu.be/DPgpCUR92bU  

## Purpose  
This trial documents an introductory attempt to regain conscious control over abdominal regions through inward contraction rather than outward flexing.  
The goal is to rebuild mind–body connection, improve somatic awareness, and begin restoring physical control that has been disrupted or lost.

Videos will be posted as progression logs throughout this process.

## Method  
During this session, inward abdominal force was applied to contract the abdomen.  
This approach emphasizes internal engagement rather than pushing outward to “flex,” allowing clearer isolation of:

- the sides (obliques)  
- the upper abdominal region  
- the central region  
- the lower abdominal region  

Breathing cycles (inhale/exhale) were used to observe how each section responds and how awareness shifts with controlled contraction.

## Observations  
- Body alignment appears uneven or “out of place,” which is expected during early somatic retraining.  
- Inward contraction provides clearer isolation of deep abdominal sections compared to outward flexing.  
- Independent control of each region is emerging but not yet consistent.  
- Visual feedback from the video helps identify asymmetries and areas needing refinement.  
- Some movements appear unstable or irregular, reflecting the current state of somatic disruption.

## Next Steps  
Future trials will focus on isolating **one abdominal region at a time**, including:

- left side  
- right side  
- upper region  
- central region  
- lower region  

Each trial will be documented separately to track progression, stability, and regained control over time.

## Notes  
This is a personal somatic recovery process.  
The body may look uneven, unstable, or misaligned in early videos — this is part of the journey.  
These trials exist to show real progress, not polished results.
