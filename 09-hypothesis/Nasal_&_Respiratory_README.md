H-003-01: Respiratory Attention Redistribution Hypothesis
Statement
Conscious attention may alter the perceived location, intensity, and pathway of respiratory sensations.
As practice increases, awareness may shift from the nostrils and upper airway toward deeper respiratory structures, creating the perception of different breathing mechanics despite the underlying physiology remaining unchanged.
---
H-003-02: Perceived Nasal Dominance Hypothesis
Statement
Individuals may experience persistent subjective dominance of one nostril during breathing.
This perceived dominance may influence:
Airflow awareness
Sensitivity to environmental contact
Breathing comfort
Somatic perception during respiratory exercises
Further observations are required to determine consistency and repeatability.

---

Alternative Explanations
Potential explanations that should be considered include:
Differences in attentional focus
Temporary nasal congestion
Natural changes in nasal airflow
Variations in sensory sensitivity
Expectation effects
Changes in posture during observation
Differences in tissue sensitivity between nasal regions
