#### 002: Attention-Assisted Post-Injection Tissue Awareness Hypothesis

Statement

Focused somatic contraction and sustained attention may increase awareness of localized tissue sensations following an injection.
The resulting sensations may be interpreted as fluid movement, tissue response, pressure changes, muscular activation, vascular activity, immune response, or other physiological processes.

---

Alternative Explanations

Several alternative explanations should be considered:


* Application of only inward force or negative feedback could have possibly been wrong. If the actual fluid is meant to be absorbed by certain surrounding areas at the site of injection.
* Increased attention and body awareness following injection.
* Local inflammatory response.
* Muscle tension changes.
* Changes in circulation.
* Increased perception of normal tissue activity.
* Expectation effects.
* Neural attention mechanisms.
* Additional experimentation is required before attributing observations to any specific process.
