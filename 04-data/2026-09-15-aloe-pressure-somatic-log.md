## 2. Experimental Protocols

### Protocol A: Topical Aloe Friction (Bilateral Alternation)
* **Timing:** Approx. 2.5 hours into the session (during a rest break).
* **Execution:** Opened aloe pads/leaves, rubbing direct friction onto one arm until the gel began to dry/goo. Washed the arm, then used that clean hand/arm to apply aloe to the opposite limb.

* **Phase 1 (Outward Pressure):** Applied manual outward pressure during a subsequent break, held for **10 full breaths**.
* **Phase 2 (Inward Pressure):** Applied manual inward pressure ~1 hour post-Phase 1, held for **10 full breaths**.
